Best Time to Visit - October to April

The best time to visit Raja Ampat is during the dry season when the seas are calm and underwater visibility is best for diving and snorkeling.

Raja Ampat: Indonesia's Underwater Paradise

Tucked away in the remote waters of West Papua, Raja Ampat is often hailed as the crown jewel of Indonesia's marine wonders and for good reason. This archpelago of over 2,500 small islands, cays, and shoals sits at the heart of the Coral Triangle, home to the richest marine biodiversity on the planet.

Divers and snorkelers travel from all corners of the globe to explore Raja Ampat's vibrant coral reefs, home to more than 1,700 species of fish and 75% of the world's known coral species. Beneath the surface, you'll find everything from tiny pygmy seahorses to majestic manta rays gliding through crystal-clear waters.

Above the waterline, the scenery is just as breathtaking. Towering limestone karsts rise dramatically from turquoise lagoons, best admired from the iconic Piaynemo or Wayag viewpoints. Hidden beaches, untouched jungles, and traditional Papuan villages complete the picture of this untouched tropical paradise.

Whether you're an experienced diver chasing the perfect underwater shot or a traveler simply seeking remote, unspoiled beauty, Raja Ampat offers an experience unlike anywhere else on Earth, a true testament to Indonesia's natural wonder.

Best Raja Ampat Spot: Piaynemo

It is known for its dramatic landscape of sheer limestone hills rising out of clear, turquoise water. Visitors often climb a wooden stairway to the top of the viewpoint for a panoramic view of the scattered islets.
